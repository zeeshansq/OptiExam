Column Specification Reference

Required header schema for batch ingestion.

question_type: MCQ_SINGLE, MCQ_MULTIPLE, IMAGE_MCQ, SHORT_ANSWER, LONG_ESSAY

prompt: Full text of the question or task.

points: Numerical score (e.g. 2.0, 5.0).

negative_points: Penalty points (e.g. 0.5, 0.0).

difficulty: EASY, MEDIUM, HARD

blooms_level: REMEMBER, UNDERSTAND, APPLY, ANALYZE, EVALUATE, CREATE

options: Choices separated by pipe: A) Opt 1 | B) Opt 2 | C) Opt 3

correct_options: Correct letter(s): A or A, C

rubric_criteria: Criteria with points: Methodology: 3.0 | Output: 2.0
